DRK Institute of Science and Technology is one of the most reputed institutions offering quality technical education in Telangana.

The following is the admission criteria:

70% Admissions under Convenor Quota

To seek admission into the first year of the four-year B.Tech course in any branch, candidates must satisfy the following requirements:

A student must have passed the Intermediate Examination of the Board of Intermediate Education, Government of Telangana with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as optional subjects, or any other examination recognized by the Government of Telangana as equivalent.

All eligible candidates for admission into the First Year must qualify in the EAMCET Examination conducted by the Government of Telangana. The candidates will be admitted by EAMCET Convenor strictly in accordance with the rank secured at the Entrance Examination and as per the reservation rules applicable by the Government.

MANAGEMENT QUOTA

30% of the total seats will be considered under the Management Quota.

To seek admission under Management Quota, candidates must satisfy the following requirements:

  • A student should either have obtained a rank or should have secured a minimum aggregate of 50% in group subjects (Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry) in the Intermediate Examination of the Board of Intermediate Education, Government of Telangana or any equivalent examination recognized by the Government.
  • 30% of the total seats will be filled under Management Quota.
  • Note: No reservation or concession will be applicable under Management Quota.
